# Conclusion

## A Codebase of Fewer Than 4,000 Lines

WireGuard has fewer than 4,000 lines of code, far less than IPsec or OpenVPN. This makes finding vulnerabilities and maintaining the code easier.

## Multiple Platforms

Running in kernel space gives WireGuard high performance. Its small codebase supports use across a wide range of devices and security properties that can remain trustworthy far into the future.

## Cryptokey Routing

Instead of complex configuration, WireGuard allows connections using only a peer’s **public key and allowed IP addresses**. This simplifies administration and helps prevent security incidents.

## Security Through Silence

WireGuard does not normally respond to unauthenticated packets, making network scanning harder and improving security.
